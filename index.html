About melo stay

Seoul outside. melo inside.

Seoul doesn't slow down. Markets spill onto the sidewalks, traffic never really stops, and Dongdaemun hums at all hours. melo stay sits right in the middle of it — and was built to feel like nothing outside exists once you're through the door.

The name says it plainly: stay calm, stay melo. It's the phrase printed at our entrance, and it's the idea behind every room — warm stone, natural oak, quiet light, nothing extra.

We opened in 2026 with one goal: give travelers visiting Korea a private, comfortable, unfussy place to land. No breakfast rush, no long check-in lines — just 24 rooms, a shared kitchen, and a neighborhood worth stepping back out into whenever you're ready.

Whether you're here for a night or a week, melo stay is designed to get out of your way and let Seoul do the rest.

Attention to detail

Laundry facilities, a relaxed guest lounge, genuine Han River-style ramen machines, and all the little amenities that make a stay feel considered — done properly.
