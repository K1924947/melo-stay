Check-in is from 15:00 to 22:00. Check-out is before 11:00.

Choose your dates on this page to see prices, then send an enquiry. A direct booking is 10% below the Booking.com price. Rooms can also be booked on Booking.com, Trip.com and Agoda.

Yes. All 24 rooms are private, with a private ensuite bathroom. There are no dorms. There are eight room types.

Every room has a private ensuite bathroom, a mini-fridge, air conditioning, a TV and a bathroom amenity kit. Most rooms also have a desk. An in-room hairdryer is provided. Free Wi-Fi is available in every room and in the common areas. Rooms use digital keycard entry.

Breakfast is not included. A shared kitchen is open to all guests, with coffee, snacks and a ramen corner, including Han River-style ramen machines.

There is a laundry room, a guest lounge, free Wi-Fi throughout, digital keycard entry and an air purifier. A 24-hour convenience mart is on the ground floor of the building.

Yes. Luggage can be left before check-in or after check-out.
